## Method

Each record was checked independently against current first-party product pages,
documentation, canonical repositories and licence files, pricing or subscription terms,
lifecycle notices, and repository metadata. Licensing follows the exact catalogue adoption
identity: commercial platforms do not inherit licences from public agents, charts, plugins, or
SDKs, while standalone projects use their canonical repository licence. Product-family,
component, and lifecycle boundaries were kept explicit; unresolved identity was retained for
review. Existing `legacy:` provenance was preserved and current sources were appended. All
reviewed records use the actual review date without manipulating ranking.

Full-catalogue duplicate checks compared names, former names, official and repository URLs,
vendors, acquisitions, and semantic overlap. The only adjacent JFrog canonical identity found
was `jfrog-artifactory`; the official current portfolio was also checked for Xray, Curation,
Release Lifecycle Management, Edge, CLI, and ML products. No record was deleted, merged, or
given a new stable ID.
