# v0.2.0 Maintenance Wave 1 Review Plan

Baseline: 1,092 canonical records, 30 taxonomy categories, 1,085 needs-review records, 7 archived records, and 35 strict link-review items in the current ledger.

Scope: select a balanced cohort of 100 high-value records for evidence-backed review without rebuilding the catalogue, reimporting the archive, or mutating canonical records in this wave.

Selection method: keep 10 records from each of 10 priority-focused categories. The set is deterministic: within each category, rank needs-review records by repository URL presence, official URL presence, source count, category breadth, and role coverage, then take the top 10.

Adjustment log: the earlier size-only cohort was replaced to better represent the requested operating profile. Documentation-learning, developer-experience, MLOps, and experimental categories were dropped in this wave so the cohort can include infrastructure-as-code, GitOps/release-promotion, cloud-platforms, and FinOps coverage while keeping the total at 100 records.

Expected evidence standard: confirm identity from the official site and repository, then look for stable maintenance signals such as documentation, release history, tags, README usage, or upstream governance notes. Do not change lifecycle or license fields unless the evidence is conclusive. This plan does not itself verify or promote records.
