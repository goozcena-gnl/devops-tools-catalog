## Method

Product-owned websites and documentation were preferred, followed by canonical
upstream repositories and their licence files, vendor pricing or terms, and
official lifecycle notices. A public component repository was not treated as
proof that its surrounding service was open source. Managed proprietary service
records were classified as commercial only when vendor pricing, billing, or terms
established that boundary; no SPDX identifier was added to those records.

Lifecycle was based on current provider notices, not URL reachability. Maturity
was assigned conservatively and independently of age or commercial status. A
record was cleared only when identity, licence, lifecycle, and maturity were all
adequately resolved. Legacy provenance was preserved. Material ambiguity remains
explicit for AITMPL Agents because the catalogue identity is the agent-component
directory while the containing project and redistributed components do not share
one uniform licence boundary.
