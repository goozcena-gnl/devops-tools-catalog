## Method

Review used official current product or project sites first, followed by official
documentation, canonical repositories and licence files, pricing and licensing pages,
lifecycle notices, acquisition or rebrand announcements, and official repository metadata.
Third-party material was not used to decide metadata. Existing `legacy:` provenance was
preserved and fresh primary sources were added.

Licence decisions follow the adoption identity. A commercial platform does not inherit the
licence of an agent, SDK, CLI, integration, uploader, runner, or upstream component. Under
existing catalogue precedent, freemium proprietary platforms remain `commercial`;
`free-saas` is reserved for a hosted identity whose primary boundary is a genuinely free
service. SPDX identifiers were added only for exact standalone software identities.

Lifecycle changes require explicit first-party evidence. Codiga's own shutdown notice is
decisive; acquisition or redirects alone would not have been. Product-family lifecycle is
kept distinct from edition lifecycle, so Confluence remains active while its Data Center
edition approaches announced end of life. Where the adoption identity cannot be licensed
without conflating adjacent components, the record remains `needs-review` as an
`IDENTITY_BLOCKER`. Unknown values are retained rather than inferred.
