# Licensing and attribution

## Repository-owned material

The repository's maintenance code, tests, documentation, and original catalog
metadata are made available under the [MIT License](../LICENSE) to the extent
that `goozcena-gnl` owns the relevant rights.

The initial catalog was migrated from earlier material in this repository.
`migration/source-inventory.json`, `migration/source-entries.json`, and
`migration/reconciliation.csv` preserve that source history and the mapping to
canonical records. They are evidence of provenance; they are not a claim of
ownership over third-party projects or linked content.

## Third-party boundaries

The catalog names and links to third-party projects, products, documentation,
and licence files. Those projects retain their own copyrights, licences, terms,
and trademarks. Inclusion does not transfer those rights, imply endorsement,
or place third-party software under this repository's MIT License.

Short factual descriptions and classification fields are provided for
discovery and engineering comparison. Follow the cited primary sources and the
upstream project's current terms before adopting, redistributing, or procuring
a tool. A catalog `license_spdx` value describes reviewed upstream evidence; it
does not grant a licence on behalf of that upstream owner.

## Corrections

If a description, attribution, licence classification, or source boundary is
incorrect, use the metadata-correction issue template or submit a focused pull
request with authoritative evidence. For a sensitive legal or security concern,
follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.
