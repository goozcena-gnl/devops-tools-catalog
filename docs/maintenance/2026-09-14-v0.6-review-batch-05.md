## Ranking policy

Candidates are ordered by `priority_score` descending, semantically parsed `verified_on`
ascending, then canonical ID ascending. The selected rows have equal scores and dates, and their
IDs are in ascending order. This is the ranking merged and post-merge verified before selection;
no candidate was manually skipped.

## Method

Review used current official product or project sites, official documentation, canonical
repositories and licence files, pricing or terms, lifecycle and rename notices, and official
repository metadata. Exact adoption identity controlled licensing. Managed services and
commercial platforms did not inherit licences from CLIs, SDKs, agents, frameworks, runners, or
other open components. Standalone projects used only their own canonical repository licence.
Legacy provenance was preserved and current primary sources were appended.

Duplicate checks compared current and former names, normalized official and repository URLs,
vendors, and obvious product overlap across the full canonical catalogue. No exact name, official
URL, or repository collision was found for the selected records.
