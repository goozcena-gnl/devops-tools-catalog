## Semantic boundaries

- Chaos Mesh has a Kubernetes CRD/controller/daemon architecture, including supported external targets; ChaosBlade has a core CLI and separate executors/operator.
- Chaos Toolkit core licensing and maintenance do not cover every extension.
- Kube Monkey deletes selected Kubernetes pods; Netflix Chaos Monkey schedules Spinnaker-managed instance/container termination. They have different deployment and recovery assumptions.
- Pumba executes against Linux-container runtimes; deploying it as a DaemonSet does not create a Kubernetes-native CRD controller.
- Toxiproxy changes proxied TCP streams; it does not provide universal packet/network impairment.
- stress-ng generates resource pressure and is explicitly unsuitable as a precise benchmark suite.
- iPerf3 measures network throughput between endpoints; it is not fault injection, iPerf2 or an application-performance guarantee.
- Capability, RBAC, namespace filters and attractive UIs do not authorize production operation or guarantee safety.
