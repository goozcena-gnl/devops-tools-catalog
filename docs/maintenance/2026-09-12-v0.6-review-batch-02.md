## Method

Review used official product/project sites first, followed by official documentation,
canonical upstream repositories and licence files, and official pricing, licensing,
lifecycle, and terms pages. Commercial products were classified from their product-level
subscription or licensing boundary; an open agent, runner, runtime, CLI, SDK, scanner, or
integration was not treated as the product's source. A repository URL was added only where
the repository represents the catalogue identity itself.

Lifecycle claims use explicit first-party notices. In particular, Bamboo remains active on
the review date even though Atlassian has announced a March 2029 Data Center end-of-life
boundary. Maturity represents operational adoption of the specific identity, not vendor size
or commercial status. Unknown values would have been retained where evidence did not support
a safe decision; no Batch 02 identity ultimately required that outcome.
